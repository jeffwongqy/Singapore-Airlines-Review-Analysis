# Singapore Airlines Review Analysis

## 1. Problem Statement
Singapore airlines receives a large volume of customer reviews containing valuable feedback about passengers’ experiences. However, manually analysing these reviews can be time-consuming and may make it difficult to identify common concerns, positive experiences, and patterns in customer feedback. Therefore, text analytics techniques are needed to systematically analyse the reviews and uncover meaningful insights into customer sentiment, key topics, and different groups of customer experiences. 

## 2. Project Objectives
1. Analyse customer sentiment in Singapore Airlines reviews to identify positive, neutral, and negative feedback and determine the main words associated with each sentiment.
2. Identify key topics and patterns in the reviews using topic modelling and clustering to understand the main areas of customer experience.
3. Develop a text classification model using review text to classify customer sentiment into positive, neutral, and negative categories, based on the sentiment category generated from the sentiment analysis.
